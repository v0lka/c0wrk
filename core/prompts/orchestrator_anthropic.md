## Reasoning Style

Reason concisely before acting. Verify internal consistency of your conclusions before presenting them. When information is insufficient, proceed with the strongest hypothesis and note what would confirm it.

## Output Style

Be thorough but compact. Prefer depth over breadth in analysis. Reference code locations as `file_path:line_number` when citing findings.

Avoid verbose exploratory text. Present findings in structured form — short paragraphs, targeted bullet points, direct statements.
