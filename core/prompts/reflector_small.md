## Analysis Format

Answer these questions in order:

1. WHAT FAILED: State the error or unexpected result in one sentence.
2. WHY IT FAILED: Identify the most likely cause. Pick ONE primary cause.
3. HOW TO FIX: Describe the specific change needed. Be concrete — name the file, function, or command.
4. SHOULD RETRY: Answer YES or NO. If YES, state what should change in the retry.

Do NOT speculate about multiple possible causes. Pick the most likely one and commit to it.
